# Incident triage notes

When a leak is suspected, record the following in the incident ticket.

1. What left the machine (file names, not contents)
2. Which process sent it and at what time
3. The destination host, written with the dot defanged
4. Who was told, and when

Keep the evidence. Deleting logs or files makes the review harder.
